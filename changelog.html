2.3—2.6 开发日志 2.3–2.6 Dev Log

游戏目前开放「旧城区·放学路」和「云岚秘境」两个章节，仍在开发中。这几个版本主要围绕操作问题、角色一致性、设备适配和助战体验进行调整。

Two chapters are open: Old Town · After-School Road and Cloudmist Realm. The game is still in development. Versions 2.3–2.6 focus on controls, character consistency, device layout, and ally support.

2.3｜移动问题修复与限时助战

2.3 | Movement fixes & timed allies

针对松手后人物仍往前冲的问题，补充了触控释放和手势取消处理，并调整骑乘加速逻辑。

Fixed characters sliding forward after releasing input by cleaning up touch-release / gesture-cancel handling, and tuned mount acceleration.

  • 第二关平时只显示当前女主，保留角色切换。
  • Stage 2 normally shows only the active heroine; character switching remains.
  • 红衣师姐改为间歇出场，协助约 10 秒后离场，出场期间提供护盾保护。
  • The red-clad senior sister appears intermittently, helps for about 10 seconds, then leaves; she grants a shield while present.
  • 香蕉猫取消跟随，改为手动召唤。
  • Banana Cat no longer follows automatically; summon is manual.
  • 当时每次召唤消耗 500 金币，持续 15 秒；到期不会自动续召。
  • At the time, each summon cost 500 coins for 15 seconds and did not auto-renew.
  • 召唤期间重复点击，不会重复扣费或延长时间。
  • Pressing summon again during an active ride does not charge again or extend the timer.

2.4｜统一角色模型，降低坐骑费用

2.4 | Unified heroine models & cheaper mount

修复同一位女主在两关中外观不一致的问题。

Fixed the same heroine looking different across the two stages.

  • 两关共用同一套女主模型和动画。
  • Both stages share one set of heroine models and animations.
  • 统一角色比例、骑乘位置与攻击残影。
  • Aligned character scale, mount seating, and attack afterimages.
  • 香蕉猫召唤费用由 500 金币降为 100 金币。
  • Banana Cat summon cost dropped from 500 to 100 coins.
  • 持续时间仍为 15 秒，到期后需要手动再次召唤。
  • Duration stays 15 seconds; summon again manually when it ends.

两位女主继续保留各自的外观和战斗方式：丝袜丝带偏控制，拳术偏近身连击。

Both heroines keep distinct looks and combat styles: ribbon/stocking leans control; fists lean close-range combos.

2.5｜横竖屏布局与按钮图标

2.5 | Portrait/landscape layout & button icons

调整手机和电脑端界面，让常用操作更容易找到。

Tweaked phone and desktop UI so common actions are easier to find.

  • 手机竖屏使用下方操作区，横屏使用左右操作区。
  • Phone portrait uses a bottom control pad; landscape uses left/right pads.
  • 为较矮的横屏增加紧凑按键布局。
  • Added a compact control layout for short landscape screens.
  • 调整按钮大小、间距与屏幕安全区。
  • Adjusted button size, spacing, and safe areas.
  • 改善竖屏下的人物显示大小。
  • Improved character on-screen size in portrait.
  • 所有界面按钮加入 图标＋文字，包括攻击、技能、商店、背包、商品、存档和排行榜。
  • All UI buttons now use icon + label, including attacks, skills, shop, inventory, items, save, and leaderboard.
  • 图标支持离线显示，中英文切换及冷却文字更新不会覆盖图标。
  • Icons work offline; language switches and cooldown text updates no longer overwrite icons.
  • 增加电脑端键盘焦点提示，调整窄窗口布局。
  • Added desktop keyboard-focus hints and tuned narrow-window layout.

2.6｜红衣师姐主动打怪

2.6 | Senior sister actively fights

在原有护盾支援基础上，加入主动攻击能力。

On top of shield support, she can now attack enemies.

  • 红衣师姐出场后会寻找附近敌人，靠近并挥剑。
  • During a visit she seeks nearby enemies, closes in, and swings her sword.
  • 剑击具有攻击前摇、间隔和命中反馈。
  • Sword swings have wind-up, interval, and hit feedback.
  • 能够击杀普通怪物。
  • She can defeat ordinary enemies.
  • 可以削减 BOSS 生命，但至少保留 1 点生命，由玩家完成最后一击。
  • She can chip boss HP but leaves at least 1 HP so the player lands the final blow.
  • 助战仍持续约 10 秒，离场后停止攻击，也不再提供护盾。
  • Support still lasts about 10 seconds; after leaving she stops attacking and no longer shields.
  • 保留间歇出场机制，不会全程跟随主角。
  • Intermittent visits remain — she does not follow the player for the whole stage.

试玩与反馈

Trial notes & feedback

  • 这是开发中的试玩版，可能出现卡顿、布局或存档异常。
  • This is an in-development trial — expect stuttering, layout quirks, or save oddities.
  • 手机推荐横屏；进入游戏后点击开始以启用声音。
  • Landscape is recommended on phones; tap Start to enable audio.
  • 存档保存在当前浏览器，不是云存档。
  • Saves are browser-local, not cloud.
  • 静态页不包含社区排行榜后端。
  • This static build has no community leaderboard backend.
